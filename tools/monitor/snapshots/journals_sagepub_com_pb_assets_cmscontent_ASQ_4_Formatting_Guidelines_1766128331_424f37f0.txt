ASQ Formatting Guidelines
File Types
ScholarOne accepts submissions in Word or pdf format. ASQ requires Word for accepted
manuscripts, as we provide in-house editing prior to submitting articles to the publisher for
layout and production. If you submit a pdf initially, please be prepared to convert your
manuscript to Word if it is accepted.
Text, Notes, and Headings
Submit text that is double spaced in 12-point Times New Roman. Do not try to reduce your page
count by adjusting the line spacing, such as by setting it to exactly 24 points. Paragraphs should
be left-aligned.
Offset quotes (those at least three lines long) and reference lists may be singled spaced.
ASQ does not print epigraphs: offset quotes that start the body of a paper.
Use footnotes sparingly. Essential material should be incorporated in the text; material with weak
relevance should be deleted. Whenever possible, place footnotes at the end of sentences, not in
the middle.
Organize the manuscript by using primary, secondary, and tertiary headings, rather than
numbered headings. Indicate the heading level as follows:
LEVEL-ONE HEADING
Paragraph text . . .
Level Two Heading
Paragraph text . . .
Level-three heading. Paragraph text . . .
Figures and Tables
In text, provide at least one direct reference to each figure and table, as well as a note that
indicates where that element should appear in the article. Title each figure and table, and for
graphs, label all axes. Figures and tables can appear at the end of your submission file, or (for a
first-round submission) you can embed them in the text if you prefer.
For accepted submissions, all tables and figures must be grouped together and placed after your
text and reference list. At that stage, tables must be provided in an editable format, and if figures
can be shared in an editable format, we will ask that you do so. (Figures created in Stata can be
sent to ASQ in a pdf or other format to be shared with the typesetter.)
For significance levels in tables, ASQ uses the following presentation:
• p < .05; •• p < .01; ••• p < .001.

We advocate against showing a significance level of p < .10. If you do include that level, indicate
it with a plus sign (+).
For notes in tables, we use the following symbols:
* First note in the table (such as one that applies to the entire table).
† Second note.
‡ Third note.
§ Fourth note.
For figures, keep the following in mind:
• ASQ still prints hard copies of issues, and the legibility of figures on the printed page is a
key consideration for figures’ layout.
• Text-heavy figures can be a challenge for legibility; try to minimize the text in any figure.
• Font sizes should be consistent within a figure. If a figure features heading text that is
much larger than other text, the smaller text may become illegible when the figure is
sized to fit on the printed page.
• Figures will appear in grayscale in the printed ASQ issue. Online versions of ASQ articles
can feature color images, but each figure also needs to make sense when it’s printed in
grayscale. Avoid text references to a specific color in the figure, as not all readers will see
that color. Use graphic patterns in addition to (or in lieu of) colors to distinguish between
elements in a figure.
Citations
Cite sources by noting in parentheses the last name of the author, the year of publication, and
page numbers for direct quotations or to refer to a point in a book. Use APA style. Details on
APA style for in-text citations can be found here.
Reference List
At the end of the manuscript text, list every reference cited in the manuscript; do not include any
references that are not cited in the text. Alphabetize this list by the (first) author’s last name. If
the list includes more than one source by a given (first) author, list that author’s references in
chronological order. Do not number references or use “ibid.,” “op. cit.,” or “loc. cit.” Also,
include URLs in reference list entries only when the URLs are needed for readers to locate the
publication. For example, if a journal article lists volume and page numbers, no URL should be
included.
