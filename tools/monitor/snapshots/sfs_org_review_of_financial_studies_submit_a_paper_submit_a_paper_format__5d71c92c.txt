Submission Checklist
My manuscript PDF:-is anonymous (does not include author information or acknowledgments)-includes the paper title-includes the abstract (limit: 100 words)-is double-spaced (limit: 28 lines per page)-has size 11 or larger font-includes the internet appendix, labeled clearly, with author information removed. (This step may be skipped if you do not have an internet appendix.)-includes alt text underneath each figure legend. For guidance on preparing alt text, see here.
My title page PDF:-includes the paper title-includes the name, affiliation, and email address for each author.
(For resubmissions only) My reply to referee PDFs:-are labeled with the referee number (example: Reply to R1)-are anonymous (do not include author information).
