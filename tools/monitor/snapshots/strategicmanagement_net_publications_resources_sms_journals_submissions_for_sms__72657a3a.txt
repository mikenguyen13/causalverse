SMS is proud to publish three leading journals that are central to its mission of fostering and supporting rigorous and practice-engaged strategic management research. These journals publish top-quality work applicable to researchers and practitioners in the field. The Strategic Management Journal, Strategic Entrepreneurship Journal, and Global Strategy Journal publish papers that are selected through rigorous double-blind review processes. Prominent scholars hold Co-Editor and Associate Editor positions and participate in the journals’ Editorial Review Boards. These leaders review all submissions, work with authors to improve their papers, and make the final decisions to accept or reject the work.
Author Guidelines
Please observe the SMJ Author Guidelines, SEJ Author Guidelines, and GSJ Author Guidelines. These documents contain information about the SMS journals’ submission processes, editorial policies, ethical policies, style, and more. Adhering to these guidelines will help manuscripts move expediently through the review and copyediting process.

Open Access
Wiley, the publisher of SMS’s journals, has numerous transformation agreements that mean research can be published Open Access at no charge to authors. Open Access means more readership and higher citation rates.
To take advantage, authors should choose the Corresponding Author wisely when submitting to the Journal. If the Corresponding Author is affiliated with a university/country with which Wiley has an agreement, the paper is eligible for this benefit.
