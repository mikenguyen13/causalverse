Submission Guidelines
This page describes the instructions for submitting papers to Econometrica. Please review the journal’s Editorial Procedures and Policies before submission.

1. Eligibility and Submission Link
To submit a paper to Econometrica, you must be a member of the Econometric Society. If you are not currently a member, please go to the Econometric Society's membership page and purchase a membership at:
https://www.econometricsociety.org/membership/join-renew
Submissions must present original, unpublished work that is not under consideration elsewhere. A working paper version should be publicly available, such as on a personal website or other public repository.
Econometrica accepts only electronic submissions.
Click here to start an online submission.
2. Submission and Publication Fees
A submission fee of US$125 for regular members and US$50 for student members applies to all new submissions. Invited resubmissions require only membership. The submission fee must be paid by credit card at the time of submission. Submission fees are non-refundable, including for papers rejected without review.
Individuals residing in countries classified as low-income by the World Bank are exempt from submission fees. The list of these countries is available here. To be charged the student rate or to be granted the exemption for low-income countries, all coauthors of the paper must meet the relevant criteria for a discount or exemption to be granted.
Accepted papers will incur a publication fee of 50% of the production cost per page. This fee applies to all typeset material and will be collected after the final proofs are approved but before publication. It will be invoiced at the prevailing rate at the time of publication.
Individuals residing in countries classified as low-income or lower-middle-income by the World Bank are exempt from publication fees. The list of these countries is available here.
3. Format and Length
Papers should be submitted in PDF format. It is recommended, though not required, that authors use the LaTeX template available from the journal’s support page:
https://www.e-publications.org/es/support/
Papers should be limited to 45 pages, including references and appendices. The font size must be at least 12 points, line spacing at least 1.5, and all margins at least 1.25 inches. These rules apply to the entire manuscript, including references and appendices.
Material should be organized to enhance readability; for example, footnotes and figures should not be placed at the end of the document. Pages should be numbered, and the title page should include an abstract of no more than 150 words, keywords, and full author affiliations (JEL codes are optional).
Econometrica does not allow the use of \textcircled{r} to indicate a certified random order for authors’ names. Authors are free to specify the methods used to determine author order in a footnote.
Authors may include a supplemental appendix with non-essential additional results and robustness analyses that are not critical to the main paper. The main paper, including any in-paper appendices but excluding the supplemental appendix, must be self-contained. The supplemental appendix should be clearly labeled, should not exceed 25 pages, and must follow the same formatting requirements (font size, line spacing, and margins) as the main text. Supplemental appendices are posted under “Supplemental Material” and are not typeset.
Exceptions to the page limits may be granted upon request. Authors seeking to submit a paper exceeding the allowed length must obtain permission from the Editor via email before submission.
4. Cover Letter
A cover letter should be included only if it contains information relevant to the review process. Specifically:
If the author has submitted related work elsewhere, they must provide Econometrica with the details.
If the submission relates to a previously rejected submission to Econometrica, the author should explain why it is sufficiently different to warrant independent review.
Any request for exemption from the journal’s Editorial Procedures and Policies must be stated explicitly at the time of initial submission.
5. Supplemental Material
As noted in Section 3, authors may submit a supplemental appendix of up to 25 pages, following the same formatting requirements as the main text.
Authors should review the Replication Policy and Experimental Papers Policy, which require submissions with empirical, experimental, or simulation results to include additional supporting documentation. This documentation may be submitted with the main paper via the online submission form and does not count toward page limits. Large files should be compressed into a .zip format. If submitting more than two supplemental files, combine them into a single .zip file.
6. Submitting a Correction or Comment
The Econometric Society journals accept short articles in the form of corrections or comments. These articles address a paper previously published in the relevant journal and aim to amend or elucidate its findings and message. Both types of articles serve to clarify the original contribution, but they differ in scope and ambition. A correction is a very short article that identifies an error or flaw in a previously published paper. The flaw must be significant enough to merit formal documentation. In most cases, the primary aim of a correction is to restore factual accuracy, not to reinterpret the original study. A comment is a more substantial and ambitious article than a correction. It may arise when a shortcoming undermines the validity or interpretation of the original contribution. A comment not only identifies the shortcoming but also elaborates on a solution and elucidates how the message of the original paper changes. A comment need not be rooted in a mistake; it may instead offer a substantial generalization or improvement over the previously published paper while still directly building on it. Corrections are frequently (but needn't be) written by the original authors of the paper in question, sometimes with additional coauthors. Comments, on the other hand, are typically prepared by an independent group of authors. Submitting a correction or comment to an Econometric Society journal requires contacting at least one of the original authors, unless an original author is part of the submitting author team. If the original authors do not respond within two months, this should be noted in the submission. The correspondence with the original authors should be included with the journal submission. The original authors may be invited to reply to any accepted correction or comment.
Corrections should be titled “Correction to ‘[original paper title]’ by [original author(s)],” and comments should be titled “Comment on ‘[original paper title]’ by [original author(s)].”
7. Submitting a Revision
An offer to revise a paper is typically valid for 12 months from the decision date. Extensions may be granted at the discretion of the handling Co-Editor but must be requested within that 12-month period.
8. Additional Information
Submission of a paper indicates that the authors agree to abide by the journal’s Editorial Procedures and Policies.
If you have any questions, please contact the Editorial Office at [email protected].
